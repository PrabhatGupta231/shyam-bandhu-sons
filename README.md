# Shyam Bandhu & Sons
## Complete Family Fashion Showroom • Men • Women • Kids
### Strictly Offline Showroom & Digital Lookbook Platform

A fully dynamic luxury digital lookbook and offline showroom website engineered for **Shyam Bandhu & Sons** (Varanasi, India). The business is strictly offline and local—designed to drive foot traffic, in-store trial bookings, and direct WhatsApp consultations with zero e-commerce cart/checkout friction.

---

## ✦ Key Showroom Features

1. **Brand Identity & Localized Header**:
   - Primary Luxury Wordmark: **SHYAM BANDHU & SONS**
   - Tagline: `Complete Family Fashion Showroom • Men • Women • Kids`
   - Top Announcement Ticker: *Visit Our Flagship Showroom • Fresh Festive & Wedding Collections Arriving Weekly • Custom Tailoring & Fitting Available In-Store*
   - Showroom utility bar with direct call button (`+91 98390 12345`), operating hours (`10:30 AM – 9:00 PM Open All 7 Days`), address, and CMS link.

2. **Strictly Offline Navigation**:
   - `HOME | MEN'S WEAR | WOMEN'S WEAR | KIDS WEAR | GALLERY & LOOKBOOK | BLOGS & ARTICLES | STORE LOCATION & CONTACT`

3. **Complete Family Hero Campaign Carousel**:
   - 3 family campaign banners (Family Wedding, Men's Imperial Sherwanis, Kids' Royal Celebrations).
   - Direct CTA buttons: **"Explore Collections"** and **"Get Store Directions"**.

4. **Category Showcase (Men, Women, Kids)**:
   - **Men's Wear**: Sherwanis, Suits & Tuxedos, Kurta Pajama Sets, Casuals & Indo-Western.
   - **Women's Wear**: Bridal Lehengas, Kadwa Banarasi Silk Sarees, Floor-Length Anarkalis, Western & Indo-Western.
   - **Kids' Wear**: Boys' Festive Sherwanis, Girls' Twirl Lehengas, Party Frocks.
   - **Zero E-Commerce**: No cart, no wishlist, no login, no prices, no checkout.
   - Action buttons: **"Enquire on WhatsApp"** (with pre-filled outfit SKU) & **"View In-Store Details"**.

5. **Clean In-Store Detail Modal**:
   - Multi-angle gallery
   - Garment description & Karigari techniques
   - Fabric specifications (Pure Katan Silk, Velvet, Organza, Cotton-Silk)
   - Available In-Store Sizes (38, 40, 42, 44, Custom Fit)
   - In-store tailoring & master alteration guarantee
   - Direct buttons: **"Ask Availability on WhatsApp"** and **"Book In-Store Trial Appointment"**.

6. **Showroom Gallery & Instagram Feed**:
   - Real photos of store interiors, VIP bridal suites, customer wedding diaries, and styled Instagram Reels.
   - Call to action: **"Follow @ShyamBandhuAndSons"**.

7. **Fashion Articles & Style Guides**:
   - Groom styling guides, authentic Kadwa Banarasi guide, and kids' festive fashion advice.

8. **Interactive Local Contact & Google Maps**:
   - Google Maps embed placeholder with 1-click direction navigation.
   - Showroom address in Godowlia, Varanasi (near Kashi Vishwanath Gateway).
   - In-store trial suite reservation form generating instant reference codes (`#SBS-SHOWROOM-XXXX`).
   - Floating green **"Chat with Showroom"** WhatsApp button.

9. **Fully Dynamic Admin CMS Panel (`/admin`)**:
   - Built-in CMS accessible at `http://localhost:3000/admin` (or [admin.html](file:///d:/project%20for%20me/shyam%20bandhu%20and%20sons/admin.html)).
   - **Catalog Manager**: Add, edit, or delete creations across Men's, Women's, and Kids' collections without touching code.
   - **Showroom Info Manager**: Update address, phone, WhatsApp, timings, and announcement banner.
   - **Gallery Manager**: Add/Delete showroom photos and Instagram reels.
   - **Article Publisher**: Publish new styling tips and wedding trend articles.
   - All changes instantly persist in `localStorage` and update live on the storefront.
